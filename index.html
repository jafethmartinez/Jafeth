How it works

Four steps, no deposit

Tell us your times

Your ship, your dock time, your all-aboard. Or your hotel if you're staying on the island.

Pick your tour

Send the request from any tour page. It takes a minute and costs nothing.

We confirm by message

We reply on WhatsApp or email with availability, the final price and exactly where to meet.

Pay on the day

Settle up with your guide when you're picked up. Cash or card, US dollars or lempira.

Why book direct

Three siblings, one island, no middleman
